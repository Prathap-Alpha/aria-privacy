Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

ARIA Personal is a private assistant used only by its owner, on his own computer.

  • It reads its owner's Google Gmail, Calendar and Drive (read-only) and sends his own typed commands to Google Assistant to switch his own lights.
  • Google data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • No data from any other person is collected.
  • The owner can remove ARIA's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
